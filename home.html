TRAVELING TO
BARCELONA

  • Look out for a booking link from Navan to arrange your travel.
  • You’ll be flying in and out of Barcelona-El Prat International Airport (BCN), the city’s main airport, located approximately 30 minutes from Hotel Majestic.
  • Upon arrival, Summit staff will be onsite to greet you and help direct you to taxis. Please be prepared to navigate on your own to Hotel Majestic.
  • On arrival day, enjoy dinner with colleagues through our Dine Around program, offering reservations at a variety of local restaurants.
  • Watch for a “Know Before You Go” email that will include everything you need to prepare for your trip, from packing tips to local logistics.
  • The weather in January in Barcelona typically sees daytime highs around 57°F (14°C) and evening lows near 46°F (8°C). Plan to pack layers and a jacket.
  • If you plan to extend your visit before or after the Summit, we invite you to explore a curated selection of experiences in and around Barcelona here
