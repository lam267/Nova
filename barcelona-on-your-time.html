Discover More of Barcelona
For those planning to extend their time before or after the Summit, Barcelona offers an exceptional opportunity to explore one of Europe’s most vibrant and culturally rich cities. From the visionary architecture of Gaudí and the flavors of world-renowned Catalan cuisine to historic neighborhoods, coastal views, and unforgettable day trips, the city invites you to slow down and take it all in. Below is a curated selection of experiences to help you make the most of your time in Barcelona.
Sagrada Familia Tour
Perfect for architecture enthusiasts and first-time visitors eager to witness Gaudí’s iconic masterpiece—Barcelona’s most iconic landmark with breathtaking stained glass and sweeping spires.
Learn more: Sagrada Familia Official Website
Century-Old Shops & Restaurant Tour
Great for culture buffs and history lovers who enjoy exploring authentic, local traditions—this tour showcases family-run businesses in beautifully preserved modernista buildings.
Learn more: Barcelona Turisme – Unique Shops
Flamenco Show & Dinner
Ideal for fans of live performances seeking a night of Spanish passion and flavor—includes tapas, dessert, drinks, and entry to a Rotea Esguerty.
Learn more: Tablao de Carmen – Flamenco & Dinner
Tapas Walking Tour
Designed for foodies and casual explorers who want to taste their way through Barcelona’s vibrant streets—strolling through La Rambla, Boqueria Market, and Plaza Reial with two tapas stops.
Learn more: Barcelona Tapas Tour
Formula 1 Spanish Grand Prix Experience
A must for football fans and sports history lovers wanting an immersive Barça experience—explore Camp Nou, the museum, and interactive exhibits.
Learn more: FC Barcelona – Camp Nou Tour
Gaudí 4D Experience & Interactive Tour
Ideal for fans of art, design, and immersive storytelling, this 4D and augmented reality journey offers a fresh, sensory look at Antoni Gaudí’s architectural genius.
Learn more: G Experiència Official Site
Catalan Vineyard Tour & Wine Tasting
Wine lovers and nature lovers alike will appreciate a relaxing tour and tasting at a vineyard like Finca Viladellops, where scenic views and premium Catalan wines come together.
Learn more: Wine Tours at Finca Viladellops
Montjuïc Hill: Gardens, Museums & Views
Those who enjoy a mix of history, gardens, museums, and sweeping city views will find Montjuïc to be a perfect escape—lush green spaces, monumental architecture, and stunning overlooks all in one hill.
Learn more: El Parc de Montjuïc – Barcelona
Montserrat Monastery & Mountain Day Trip
Spiritual pilgrims, hikers, and art lovers will love Montserrat for its dramatic mountain landscapes, the Benedictine monastery, the Black Madonna sanctuary, and a rich museum of art amidst natural beauty.
Learn more: Montserrat – Tourism of Barcelona / Spain
